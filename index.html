Straight answers

Do I have to sign a contract?+

No. The monthly services are month-to-month — Get Found at $149, Growth System at $297. Reply “cancel” anytime and it ends at the end of that month. The Lead Machine is a one-time setup fee — it's yours whether or not you keep the optional retainer.

Who is this built for?+

Local businesses that get found on Google and booked by phone: roofers, HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, and other home-service trades. If your customers search “near me” before they call, this is built for you.

What do you need from me to start?+

Your business name, your city and service area, and access to your Google Business Profile. That's it — we handle the rest, and you approve anything public before it goes live.

How fast will I see results?+

Honestly: Google listings respond over weeks, not overnight, and anyone who promises you the #1 spot by Friday is selling you something. What you will see right away is a fully built-out listing, every review answered, and a monthly report showing your calls and searches moving.

Why start with a free audit?+

Because you shouldn't pay anyone — us included — before you've seen how your listing actually looks. The audit is one page: what's working, what's costing you calls, and what we'd fix first. Keep it either way.
